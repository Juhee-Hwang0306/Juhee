Hello, I'm Juhee! 👋

I'm a PhD student at Seoul National University and a researcher in Prof. Jae-Young Lim's research team in the Department of Rehabilitation Medicine at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital.

My research explores how digital health technologies and data-driven approaches can improve rehabilitation and health outcomes. I combine physical activity research, large-scale medical data such as nationwide health insurance claims, and AI models, including generative models for clinical time-series data, to better understand the health needs of people with disabilities and older adults and to improve prognostic prediction in rehabilitation.
